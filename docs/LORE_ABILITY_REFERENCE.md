# HWFWM ability reference — the mechanical texture we are aiming at

Gathered round 51 for the essence-identity work. Sources: the Book 12 appendix
for Jason, the fandom/breezewiki ability pages the user screenshotted for
Humphrey and Farrah's Potent abilities, and the user's own transcription of
Farrah's four essences.

The point of this file is NOT lore. It is the SHAPE of the mechanics: what an
essence ability actually does, and why the same ability on two characters is
still recognisably that essence.

## The structural rules (well evidenced)

- **Five abilities per essence.** Jason has 5 in each of four essences.
  Humphrey has 5 in each of four. Farrah has 5 in each of four. This is a hard
  rule, not a tendency.
- **Three chosen essences + one confluence = four**, and the four map ONE TO ONE
  onto the four attributes. Jason: Dark[Speed] Blood[Power] Sin[Recovery]
  Doom[Spirit]. Humphrey: Magic[Spirit] Might[Power] Wing[Speed] Dragon[Recovery].
- **The confluence is determined by the trio**, many-to-one (Doom arises from
  both Death+Potent+Void and Dark+Blood+Sin). Earth+Fire+Potent = Volcano.
- **Awakening stones are random but STEERED** by race, the essences already
  held, what the person already knows, and what has not been awakened yet.
  Rarer stones do not give stronger abilities -- they give more SPECIFIC ones.
- **Abilities have per-rank effects that ADD clauses, not just numbers.**
  Iron gives the base effect; Bronze and Silver bolt on new mechanics.

## Farrah Hurin -- Earth / Fire / Potent -> Volcano

| Essence | Abilities |
|---|---|
| Earth | Obsidian Armour, Seeking Spire, Strength of the Earth, Obsidian Shard Storm, Obsidian Wall |
| Fire | Fire Jump, Burning Orbs, Fire Strike, Fire Bolt, Eyes of Fire |
| Potent | Limit Breaker, Relentless Assault, Boost, Burning Soul, Rising Flames |
| Volcano (conf.) | Lava Cannon, Flamecore Ravager, Magma Trap, Magma Golem, Volcanic Resurgence |

Read the Earth list: armour, a wall, a spire, a shard storm, a strength buff.
It is a COMPLETE KIT SHAPE on its own -- defence, terrain, control, damage,
buff. Fire is the same shape in a different medium: mobility (Fire Jump),
orbiting damage (Burning Orbs), melee (Fire Strike), ranged (Fire Bolt),
perception (Eyes of Fire).

The confluence is not "Earth plus Fire" -- it is a THIRD THING made of both:
lava. A cannon, a trap, a golem, a resurgence, a ravager.

## Potent -- the essence that has no element of its own

This is the most instructive essence in the whole set, because it proves an
essence can be defined by what it DOES TO YOUR OTHER ABILITIES.

- **Boost** -- special ability. Increases the effect of the NEXT essence
  ability used. Self only.
- **Rising Flames** -- spell, high mana, 3 min cooldown.
  - iron: damage of every [Burning] instance YOU inflict slowly increases
  - bronze: when a [Burning] reaches maximum damage potential it DETONATES,
    consuming the instance and dealing all remaining potential at once
  - silver: detonations deal damage in an area around the victim
- **Relentless Assault** (Might/Potent) -- special attack, magic, dispel.
  Cost: LOW STAMINA, INCREASING WITH EACH SUCCESSIVE ATTACK. Cooldown: NONE.
  - iron: each use in quick succession increases this attack's damage
  - bronze: past a threshold of successive attacks, escalating resonating-force
  - silver: past the threshold, escalating disruptive-force AND dispels a boon
    from the target; subsequent attacks dispel an escalating number
- **Limit Breaker**, **Burning Soul** -- names only.

Potent's identity: *amplification, escalation, and payoff*. Nothing in it is a
plain damage number.

## Humphrey Geller -- Magic / Might / Wing -> Dragon

- **Shield Breaker** (Might) -- special attack. Low mana, moderate stamina,
  10s cooldown. Requires a HEAVY WEAPON.
  - iron: extra resonating-force damage, highly effective against physical
    defences
  - bronze: damage to rigid material significantly increased
  - silver: inflicts [Vibrant Echo] on anyone damaged -- a stacking
    damage-over-time whose instances are cumulative
- **Unstoppable Force** (Might) -- melee. High mana, EXTREME stamina, 60s.
  Requires a heavy weapon.
  - iron: massive momentum, resonating + disruptive force
  - bronze: FOR EACH ENEMY STRUCK, the cooldown AND the cost of the next use
    are reduced
  - silver: a blast wave originates from each enemy struck
- **Immortality** (Might + **Awakening Stone of Rebirth**) -- special ability
  (healing, recovery). Cost NONE. Cooldown 24 HOURS.
  - iron: instantly restore a large portion of health, mana and stamina; the
    amount restored SCALES WITH HOW DEPLETED THEY ARE
  - bronze: ongoing recovery whose strength is set by the depletion at the
    moment of activation
- **Dragon Wing Sword** (Wing) -- conjuration (fire). High mana, 60s.
  - iron: conjures a huge wing-shaped sword; special attacks WITH THE MOVEMENT
    SUBTYPE performed with this weapon deal additional damage
  - bronze: normal and special attacks with it inflict fire damage and [Burning]
- Other Might: Relentless Assault, Mighty Strength
- Magic: Crystalize Mana, Dragon Sight, Teleport, Magical Armory,
  **Spirit Reaper (Awakening Stone of the Reaper)**
- Wing: Dive Bomb, Dragon Wings, Dragon Wing Sword, Flying Leap, Razor Wing Sword
- Dragon (conf.): Dragon Armor, Fire Breath, Dragon's Might, Familiar Stash,
  **Spartoi (Awakening Stone of the Champion)** -- summons skeletal warriors

## Jason Asano -- Dark / Blood / Sin -> Doom

The affliction engine, and the clearest worked example of a kit that COMBOS.

- **Dark** applies necrotic afflictions (Hand of the Reaper: [Creeping Death],
  [Rigor Mortis], [Weakness of the Flesh]); also Midnight Eyes, Cloak of Night,
  Path of Shadows (shadow teleport), Shadow of the Hegemon (familiar).
- **Blood** applies bleed/poison and drains. Haemorrhage's [Blood From a Stone]
  **negates bleed immunity**, which is the precondition for Feast of Blood,
  which **scales with the number of poisons on the target**. Blood Harvest
  drains a CORPSE for resources and stacking boons.
- **Sin** is the inverted one, and the most interesting design lesson in the
  series: on a character whose whole identity is applying afflictions, the Sin
  essence produces ANTI-affliction abilities. Sin Eater consumes curses,
  diseases and poisons **off allies inside his aura** and pays out boons.
  Feast of Absolution cleanses allies and converts each cleanse into an ally
  boon AND an enemy debuff. Hegemony is an aura raising ally resistance and
  lowering enemy resistance.
- **Doom** (confluence) CONVERTS the stack into damage. Punition = necrotic
  damage FOR EACH AFFLICTION on the target. Verdict = execute scaling with how
  injured they are. Inexorable Doom applies [Inescapable]/[Persecution] which
  **prevent cleansing and prevent healing**, locking the stack in. Avatar of
  Doom's familiar spreads EVERY affliction on a target to other enemies.

## What we should steal

1. **Cost and cooldown are design surface, not bookkeeping.** Immortality costs
   nothing and has a 24-hour cooldown. Relentless Assault has no cooldown and a
   cost that climbs as you spam it. Unstoppable Force refunds cooldown per
   enemy hit. These are three completely different rhythms.
2. **Conditional scaling is the main lever of identity.** "Scales with how
   depleted you are", "scales with afflictions on the target", "increases with
   each successive use", "reduced per enemy struck".
3. **Abilities that key off OTHER abilities' output.** Rising Flames does
   nothing on its own -- it upgrades every [Burning] you already apply. Boost
   upgrades the next ability. Punition reads the affliction count someone else
   in the kit put there.
4. **Requirements as identity.** Shield Breaker and Unstoppable Force REQUIRE A
   HEAVY WEAPON. Dragon Wing Sword buffs only MOVEMENT-subtype attacks.
5. **The inverted essence.** Sin on an affliction character became the party's
   cleanse. An essence should be allowed to point the other way.
