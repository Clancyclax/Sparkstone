## Every companion has their story

All 41 remaining companions' story quests are built: the 4 outlaws and the 37 adventurers. Each follows its design, as follows:
- **Shape:** 4 or 5 stages, built from the existing objectives. These are going somewhere with them, asking a named townsperson, a confidence, a cull, clearing a place, recovering something, or holding against waves.
- **Climax:** the story moment, which wakes their nature evolution.
- **Afterwards:** new idle lines, trust, and a deed for the adventurers who saw it.

**How to start one:** an adventurer opens their story once they trust you 1.5 times their need, at the rank the story asks. An outlaw opens theirs after their fifth arc beat.
- The button is on their own talk: for example "Ask about the barn" on Kesh.
- Adventurers' own talk now also carries "Your martial art" (from round 406). It had been missing for recruited adventurers.

**The outlaws end three ways:** the climax reads how you answered their arc (redeemed, unsure or hardened), and so do their lines from then on.
- Lucy faces her mother. Redeemed: "I still hit people for money. I just pick them now." Hardened: she takes the band, and her mother lives.
- Jole, Ariani and Slice each have their three endings the same way.

**Where a design named something the game doesn't have, I substituted:**
- Several fights moved to a tier that suits the story's rank. For example, Panterimp only exist from tier 2, so Kesh's iron story uses Palefang.
- Some talk targets moved to a settlement that has people. Tollmarket and Gallowsreach have nobody to talk to.
- A few stories gained a stage. Calder's report goes to Expedition Lead Caro, who passes it to the Society.
- Time-of-day moments (Ilyra's noon, Orrin's night) and Wim's early tide are told, not timed.

**Prism's story stays her round-125 chain.** Her gold answer page is now her story moment:
- **Idle lines:** she gets her own lines from then on.
- **A sixth dish, Supper at Home:** restores more and buffs power, speed and crit for four minutes. Her nature (Outworlder) has no companion evolution to wake, so the dish is the reward the design called for.

## The guard contracts explain themselves

"A Day's Guard For the Cutter" and the other guard jobs now talk.

**When you arrive:** the client introduces herself and tells you:
- how many loads she needs and how long each takes, about 22 seconds a load, so about a minute all told;
- that things come out of the brush every 9 seconds or so;
- that she stops if you wander off;
- that the board pays a bonus if she is never hurt.

The work starts once you've read it, or a few seconds after.

**While she works:**
- She calls each load ("That's 2. One more load.") and warns you when something comes.
- She says when you've wandered off and when you're back.
- She calls the last load.

**When it's done:** she says so in a box ("That's the lot... Go and tell the board"), then shoulders the load and walks off home instead of vanishing.

**The same for the other two:**
- **The cart escort:** the driver says where you're going, how far, and what happens to crates. They tell you when you arrive.
- **The two stands:** the siege and the raid each say how many waves are coming and what loses it, and give a goodbye.

## Walls, gates and stairs

- **People against the walls.** A wall course took its depth from the point nearest the player, so everyone else was drawn in front of it or behind it depending on where you stood. Now each person is placed on their own side of each wall they overlap, by your hard rule: feet showing (past the wall's face) means in front; feet behind it means behind. Gate arches keep their own transition, your one exception.
- **The north gate guards** stand outside on the road, one on each side of the road's middle line, seven tiles out. This applies to every north gate, the way the west gate's guards already stood.
- **Finer stairs.** A stone flight is now treads about 5 pixels high: four to a tile, eight on a two-tile flight.
  - Each tread is flat, and walking up them steps you up.
  - The flight is drawn as those treads, with their risers and the stepped sides.
  - The cliff face of whatever stands above a flight reaches down to its lowest tread.

## Dragon's Breath is a breath

It is now a true breath attack:
- **The cone:** it hits everything in a cone in the direction you face, out to its reach, and sets fire to what it washes over.
- **The drawing:** a rolling cone of flame from your mouth, with embers flung along it.
- **Your kit:** it no longer makes a ring on the ground around you. Kits are rebuilt from your essences on load, so yours changes when you load.

## Olb's gift

- **When:** it now comes as you wake in the infirmary. It used to fire after the wake scene. If the evolution list had not finished loading, it went into the general reward queue and paid out whenever. Now the wake waits for the list (a second or two), and pays this gift itself.
- **How often:** the fourfold aura (The Soul Laid Open, an aura on every essence) is a one-in-a-hundred chance per run, and so is its perception twin. It had been first on the list, so every banked reward after Olb became it.

## Soul scars and auras

- **Every soul scar** makes your aura reach 15% further, as well as enlarging your soul.
- **With two or more auras and a scar,** your aura's strength is not split when it presses several things at once.
- **With all four auras running and a scar,** your auras reach another 25% further and count as one rank higher, both their fields and the aura contest. They stop at Gold.

The Character page's Soul Scars section says all of this. The infirmary priest mentions the wider aura.

## The essence cards

- **Every ability shows its own rank** beside its name, for example "Venom Strike · Iron 3". Hovering shows how far it is to the next.
- **Hard rule:** an essence's progress line is its lowest ability, not the average. It is 0% until all four stones are set.
